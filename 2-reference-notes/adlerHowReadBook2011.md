## **Question 1: What kind of book is this?**
A practical book with guidance and useful information on reading

## **Question 2: What is the book about as a whole?**
Information gleamed from Part I and II:  
	We don't get taught to read effectively (getting information from authors and making their ideas our own). To fix this, we must be more active and systematic in our reading habits. This system split reading into 4 distinct levels each with their own goals to understand.
#### Question 2a: What is the structural order of the work whereby the author develops his conception or understanding of that general subject?
The book introduces the four levels of reading, then subsequent chapters go further into detail about the levels. The analytical and synoptical levels are their own parts while elementary and inspectional are simply chapters. After analytical, there's a section that goes into detail about specific types of written material that may need clarification on how to apply the broad analytical reading rules.

#### Main Ideas:  
- Reading is the process of bringing the mind to higher levels of understanding  
- The amount of activity in the reading process correlates to better reading (how would "better" be defined?)  
- Active reading involves learning separate acts that eventually are performed automatically to create a single, complex habit. This complex habit is employed to answer 4 simple questions about the reading.
