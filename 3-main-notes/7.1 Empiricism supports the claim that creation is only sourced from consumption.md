Empiricism is an epistemological view which claims that knowledge or ideas can only be sourced from our experiences. Under this epistemology, producing new things can only come from consuming other things in the world.

> _The Empiricism Thesis_: We have no source of knowledge in S or for the concepts we use in S other than experience.
